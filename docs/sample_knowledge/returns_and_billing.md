# EchoStream-AI — Return Policy & Billing FAQ

## Returns

Customers may return hardware within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
Items must include all original accessories. Refunds are issued to the original
payment method within 5 business days of the warehouse receiving the return.

### How to start a return
1. Sign in to account.acme.example and open Orders.
2. Select the order and click "Return item".
3. Print the prepaid label. Drop the parcel at any partner locker.

### Exceptions
Opened networking equipment (routers, mesh nodes) can be returned, but a 10
percent restocking fee applies if the original firmware was never updated.
Consumables such as batteries are final sale unless defective.

## Billing

Subscriptions renew on the same calendar day each month. If a payment fails,
retry happens automatically after 48 hours and again after 5 days; after the
third failure the subscription is paused, not cancelled.

### Invoices
Invoices are generated on renewal and emailed as PDF. VAT IDs can be added
under Billing > Tax settings and apply to future invoices only.

### Refund timing
Card refunds appear in 3-5 business days. Wallet refunds are instant.

## Support hours

Phone support is available 08:00-20:00 Central European Time on weekdays. Chat
and voice-agent support are available around the clock. Emergency line for
business customers operates 24/7.
